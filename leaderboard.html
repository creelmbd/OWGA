Tier Assignments

Players are seeded into three tiers based on prior season results. During regular season rounds, Tier B players receive a −1 stroke handicap per round and Tier C players receive a −2 stroke handicap. Championship Day itself is scored straight up with no handicap — but entering it, Tier B and Tier C players also get a one-time Championship handicap (same −1/−2 rate) added on top of their season total, used to seed the Aug 22 pairings.

Entering Championship Day

Seeding & Pairings

Once the last regular round (Aug 21) is in, players are seeded for Aug 22 pairings by their final hdcp-adjusted season total — Tier B: −1/round, Tier C: −2/round — plus a one-time Championship handicap on top (Tier B: −1, Tier C: −2). This seeding sets pairings only; it does not change how the Championship round itself is scored.
